Contest Rules:

  • Contest Residency Requirements:
  • SlikStik Contest: US and Canadian Residents Only. (Other countries accepted if the winner is willing to pay for shipping costs.)
  • X-Arcade Trackball: Trackballs can only be shipped to North America, Australia, New Zealand and England.
  • Dream Arcades Grand Prize: US and Canadian residents only.
    (Note: A Canadian winner will need to pay $50 for the extra shipping, UPS documentation, and any import and borage fees)
  • X-Arcade Midway Games Pack: Open to all countries.
  • Alawar Pick 3 Games: Open to all countries.
  • GameRoom Magazine Giveaways: US Residents Only.
  • Rollie Electronics Translucent Joysticks: Open to all countries.
  • MAME Marquees Cabinet Artwork: Open to all countries.
  • Quasimoto QuasiCON Control Panels: US and Canadian residents only.
  • Groovy Game Gear Giveaways: US Residents Only.
  • Pop-up Contests: the pop-up contests will list their residency requirements within each contest's instructions.
  • One entry per person, please. Multiple entries will result in disqualification.
  • You can win multiple prizes (i.e., you might win a weekly giveaway and the grand prize!) The only restriction is that you may only enter each contest once (no multiple emails!)
  • You'll need to enter for each contest that you wish to win: each "selection pool" is separate.
  • Any information provided will only be shared with participating companies and will not be sold or disclosed to any third parties.
